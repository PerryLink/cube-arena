What the modes mean
Each cube is a real 3D object: six faces modelled in 3D space, three visible at a time, slowly rotating. Progress is scored across all 54 stickers.
mode · guidedThe solver knows the optimal move and proposes it. The model picks; if its pick is worse, the solver plays the optimal move instead and it is logged as corrected. The cube always gets solved.
mode · greedyOne question per turn: which single move improves the cube most right now? No subgoal, no lookahead, no plan. The unaided baseline.
mode · stagedSame one question per turn, but the model is handed a beginner-method subgoal to serve: white cross, then first layer, then two layers, then solve.
policy · sampleThe move is drawn from the model's own probability distribution. The honest use of a stochastic policy.
policy · argmaxAlways take the single most likely move. Shown for contrast: it collapses and the model repeats one move forever.
